# Release handoff: Sets page, cleaner page titles (2026-11-25)

Prepared on `mobile/dock-icons-2026-11-25` on top of the live `bd0c832`, and pushed to `main` by Claude at the client's request. Publication is the GitHub Pages workflow on every push to `main`.

## What ships

- **New `/sets` page** for recorded sets. For now it shows the title "Sets (coming soon)", with "(coming soon)" smaller in the same headline face, and the line "Full recordings of the sets from our nights. We'll be uploading them here." under it. Its room hangs a neon record button (ring and lit dot, hot red-pink).
  - **Navigation:** Sets is in the top menu, the footer and the sitemap, and sits between Archive and About along the room's wall, so the camera pans to it.
  - **Phone dock:** a sixth stop with a video icon.
- **Page titles after a page move:** no cyan ring or glow. The room's own focus style (`html.has-room :focus-visible`) had outranked the earlier reset. Phone Safari shows that focus while headless Chrome taps don't.
- **Small labels above page titles removed** on Events, Archive, About, Contact, Privacy, Terms and archive nights. The event pages' status label ("Past event" / ticket status) and "Error 404" stay.
