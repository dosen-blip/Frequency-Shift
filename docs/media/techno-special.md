# Techno Special media selection

Event: September 4, 2026, GRIDWRKS, Ottawa.

Source: [FS @ GRIDWRKS, Sept. 04, 2026](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1xX8CSa-IQOgjUTXvq1FjlLfbPm5aDjPf). Video originals are in its `videos` folder.

Public-use context: the client explicitly requested using this supplied folder for the featured website gallery on September 11, 2026. Photographer and videographer names are unconfirmed; no attribution was guessed. The client will identify the DJ running order later, so no per-set artist labels are published.

Reviewed all 132 photographs and 54 video previews in filename order, then reviewed shortlisted photos and clip sequences at larger size. Selected 16 photographs and six clips. The edit covers the early party-hat booth, leather-jacket booth, shared booth, and final booth sections, with dancefloor frames between them. These are visual selection notes, not artist identifications or confirmed set boundaries.

Priorities: strong red/cyan lighting, clear gestures, distinct compositions, laser geometry, and documentary variety. Omitted washed-out flash portraits, near-duplicates, obscured subjects, and weak compositions. No generated imagery, retouching, or artificial event reconstruction.

Original downloads remain outside public routes at `/tmp/fs-techno/source` (temporary working copies); Drive remains the source of record. Photos retain their compositions and use sRGB, metadata-free WebP derivatives at 480px, 800px, and up to 1920px on the long edge. Video excerpts retain source sound, use 1280×720 H.264/AAC, and only load when played.
